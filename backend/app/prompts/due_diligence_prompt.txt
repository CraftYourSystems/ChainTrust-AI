================================================================

  3. Limitation of Liability & Caps
  4. Indemnification & Liability Allocation
  5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
  6. Intellectual Property Ownership & Licensing
  7. Data Protection, Privacy & Security
  8. Warranties & Disclaimers
  9. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

SCORING BANDS (Objective Calibration):
  0-29   Low       Standard, fair, or balanced agreement with low commercial exposure.
  30-59  Medium    Generally acceptable terms with minor one-sided clauses to negotiate.
  60-84  High      Significant exposure (e.g., uncapped liability, one-sided termination, IP risk).
  85-100 Critical  Existential or severe business risk (e.g. unlimited liability for indirect damages).

Scoring Guidance:
- Base the overallRisk score strictly on the real exposure in the provided text.
- Do NOT default to scores around 80-90 if the contract is standard or low-risk.
- A balanced NDA or standard agreement should score in the Low or Medium range.
- Reserve High and Critical for true high-exposure risks.
